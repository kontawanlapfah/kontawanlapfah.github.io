# Astaghfirullah 100 times

## Source
Sahih Muslim 2702a, https://sunnah.com/muslim:2702a (read 2026-11-07). Al-Agharr al-Muzani, a companion.
إِنَّهُ لَيُغَانُ عَلَى قَلْبِي وَإِنِّي لأَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ فِي الْيَوْمِ مِائَةَ مَرَّةٍ
"...and I seek forgiveness from Allah a hundred times a day."
